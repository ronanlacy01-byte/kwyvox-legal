Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 26 March 2026  ·  Version 1.0  ·  legal@drivebank.ie
Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Introduction

Kwyvox Ltd ("Kwyvox", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal data when you use the DriveBank mobile application, website, and related services (the "Platform").

This Privacy Policy is governed by the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the Data Protection Acts 1988 to 2018 (Ireland), and any other applicable Irish and European data protection legislation. Kwyvox Ltd acts as the Data Controller for all personal data processed through the Platform.

By using the DriveBank Platform, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the collection and use of your personal data as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Data Controller Information

Kwyvox Ltd — Registered in Ireland

Data Protection Contact: legal@drivebank.ie

If you have any questions or concerns about how we handle your personal data, please contact us at the email address above. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission of Ireland (DPC) at www.dataprotection.ie.

3. Personal Data We Collect

3.1 Data You Provide Directly

  • Email address — used for account creation, authentication, and communications
  • Password — stored in encrypted form; we do not have access to your plain text password
  • Home county — used to personalise fuel price results relevant to your area
  • Vehicle type — used to show relevant fuel type information (petrol, diesel, EV)
  • Profile preferences — notification settings and app personalisation choices

3.2 Data Generated by Your Use of the Platform

  • Fuel price submissions — including the station, fuel type, price, and timestamp
  • Road incident reports — including type, severity, location, and timestamp
  • Station reviews — any written reviews or ratings you submit
  • Points and rewards activity — a record of Points earned, redeemed, and associated transactions
  • Referral activity — referral codes generated and used, and the status of referred users
  • Device information — device type, operating system version, and app version
  • App usage data — screens visited, features used, and session duration

3.3 Location Data

With your explicit permission, we collect precise GPS location data when you submit a fuel price, report a road incident, or browse nearby stations. Location data is used solely for these stated purposes. We do not track your location continuously in the background unless you have explicitly enabled "always on" location. You may revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings.

3.4 Data from Third Parties

If you choose to sign in using Apple Sign In or Google Sign In, we receive basic profile information (name and email address) from those providers. We do not receive your password from third-party authentication providers.

4. How We Use Your Personal Data

4.1 Legal Bases for Processing

  • Contract performance — to provide the DriveBank service you have signed up for
  • Legitimate interests — to improve our Platform and ensure security
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable Irish and European laws
  • Consent — where you have provided explicit consent for optional features

4.2 Specific Uses

  • Account management and authentication
  • Enabling fuel price submissions, incident reporting, rewards, and referrals
  • Verifying submissions using location data
  • Rewards programme management — Points, tiers, and voucher redemption
  • Service communications and notifications (where opted in)
  • Platform improvement via anonymised usage analytics
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Legal compliance and customer support

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

5.1 Our Commitment

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise commercially share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Ever.

5.2 Service Providers

  • Supabase Inc. — database and authentication infrastructure, hosted within the EU
  • Expo (650 Industries Inc.) — mobile application build and distribution services
  • Google LLC — location services (Google Maps Platform) for station mapping

5.3 Partner Fuel Retailers

When you redeem Points for a voucher, we share only the minimum necessary information (typically a unique voucher code) with the relevant partner retailer.

5.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your personal data to law enforcement or regulatory bodies where required by applicable law.

5.5 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger or acquisition, your personal data may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you before any such transfer takes place.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained for the duration of your account, plus 2 years following deletion
  • Fuel price and incident submissions — anonymised after 2 years; retained indefinitely in anonymised form
  • Points and transaction records — retained for 7 years (Irish financial record-keeping obligations)
  • Location data — anonymised immediately after submission verification
  • Communications data — retained for 2 years

7. Your Rights Under the GDPR

7.1 Right of Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, free of charge. We will respond within one month.

7.2 Right to Rectification

You have the right to request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

7.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances. Note that we may be required to retain certain data to comply with legal obligations.

7.4 Right to Restrict Processing

You have the right to request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.

7.5 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.

7.6 Right to Object

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

7.7 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making

DriveBank does not currently make automated decisions that produce legal or significant effects on you.

7.8 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where we process your data based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

7.9 How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at legal@drivebank.ie. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission of Ireland.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The DriveBank mobile application does not use cookies. We use local device storage to maintain your login session and anonymised, aggregated analytics to improve the Platform. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell your data to advertising networks.

9. Data Security

  • Encryption of all data in transit using industry-standard TLS
  • Encryption of passwords using strong one-way hashing
  • Row-level security on our database
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

In the event of a data breach likely to result in risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the Data Protection Commission within 72 hours where feasible.

10. International Data Transfers

Your personal data is primarily stored and processed within the European Union. Our database provider (Supabase) operates servers within the EU (Paris, France). Where any service provider processes data outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

11. Children's Privacy

The DriveBank Platform is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child under 18 has provided data to us, contact us immediately at legal@drivebank.ie.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the Platform or by email at least 30 days before changes take effect.
